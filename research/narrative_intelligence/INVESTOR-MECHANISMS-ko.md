## A real revision, not a market-timing signal

Marks Jan 2025: elevated equity valuation alone does not demonstrate a bubble.
Apr: credit spreads widen alongside higher default risk; intended faster fund
deployment. Aug: explicitly revises equity assessment toward greater concern,
without predicting an imminent correction. Dec: separates transformative AI
from capital loss and financing risks; also reports some data-center investment.
Feb 2026: reiterates technology vs investor return distinction and AI inference
limits. Nov: emphasizes concentrated AI exposure, compounded assumptions and
uncertainty-aware sizing. Credit opportunity and equity caution are not a
contradiction. An expressed intention is not a completed transaction. Repeated
memos from the same author are not independent expert votes.

## Observable behavior vs investment philosophy

Buffett's Feb 22 2025 shareholder letter (printed p7, visually checked) states
continued preference for ownership of good businesses while listed equity fell.
The 2024 report K-66 and K-70 visually checked. Consolidated cash flow USD millions:
2022 buys 67930, sales 33664; 2023 buys 16462, sales 40631; 2024 buys 9237,
sales 143359. Net sales -34266, 24169, 134122. Insurance and Other gross
cash-equivalents + short-term T-bills: 163887 in 2023, 330805 in 2024.
Not net of unsettled payables, not full company cash, not a portfolio weight.
These comparative values come from 2024 report; not historical live vintages.
Scope is not identical to consolidated flow. No individual security research.
Company holdings differ from price change or cash flows; tax, insurance/liquidity
needs, opportunity set and realized gains can differ from macro timing motives.
Do not infer Buffett predicts a crash or contradicts equity preference.

## Implication for Narrative Intelligence

New schema must distinguish horizon, asset, speaker, statement modality, action
intent, self-reported action, audited aggregate data and causal interpretation.
Only target-defined forecasts can be scored. Current 14/14 cards not scoreable.
Potential next test: use complete publication calendar; compare explicit
valuation/risk-assessment changes against same-date quantitative baselines
without turning ambiguity into bullish/bearish labels; establish independent
human annotation and target/falsifier first. Cross-actor agreement is not
independence when actors cite common reports. Source-only retrieval with
permissions, no bulk copyrighted text in git. No original bytes or PIT
certification. No activation or forecast registration.
