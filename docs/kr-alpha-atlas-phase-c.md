# KR Alpha Atlas Phase C — frozen executable development study

This is one finite study, with **no historical outcome access in this PR**. The
2013–2026 Korean history is previously exposed. Every eventual result is
`EXPLORATORY_DEVELOPMENT_ON_OUTCOME_EXPOSED_KR_HISTORY`; chronological tests are
not untouched confirmation. Synthetic results validate software, never real alpha.

## Contract and scope

`research_specs/kr-alpha-atlas-phase-c-v1.json` is the scientific contract. Its
exact-byte sidecar, transitive code closure, source objects, Phase B matrix digest,
result schema, original artifact lineage and protected pre-existing file hashes
are checked before authorization and again before the permanent lock. The
validator rebuilds the entire supported registration and refuses omissions,
mutations, unknown roles, unsupported horizons and changed dependency bytes.
There is no feature/horizon search, parameter grid or discretionary intermediate
outcome decision. The freeze command refuses an existing lock or result.

The authoritative Phase B manifest supplies **38** new alpha candidates. Its
family horizon assignments imply **73** readings: A22, B8, C10, D22, E4, F3, H2,
J2. Null family metadata is not a horizon. Of these, 51 have H21/H126 inferential
scope; 22 H63/H252 readings are descriptive. There are 20 baseline cells, four full
model cells, 15 add-family and 15 remove-family cells (30 paired comparisons),
73 residual-information readings and 73 conditional-slope readings. No previously
tested feature receives another individual discovery reading; its sealed study
identifiers are retained as references.

Exactly X1–X6 are retained. X1 uses the Phase B confirmation **binary metadata**,
not a percentile pretending to be a binary confirmation. X4 remains source
blocked. Its unavailable investor flow is never replaced by D11. X6 splits the
momentum spread across dates with the recorded market trend/volatility state;
the state is constant across names and cannot be a four-corner cross-section.
Its state contrast and one-year-lag negative control are descriptive, cannot
nominate an investment, and say nothing about inflation, CPI or unemployment.
That separate macro hypothesis remains explicitly untested for prospective/US work.

## Statistics and chronology

Every cross-sectional reading is formed per signal date, then averaged equally
across dates. Minimum 30 measured/valid names, three per required tercile/cell,
52 dates and 80% of the predeclared scheduled dates. The yearly stability floor is
13 dates; both chronological halves must have positive oriented estimates and
at least 60% of evaluable years must be positive. Market-state and liquidity-tier
results are published without searches over alternative boundaries.

Spearman IC and within-industry IC are descriptive Newey–West summaries, with
lag `ceil(H/5)-1` in weekly observations. Linear return/spread and paired MSE
statistics telescope their per-session contributions onto calendar weeks; their
sum must equal the per-date statistic sum within 1e-9. H21/H126 reuse the calibrated
calendar-time SN interval, critical 66.57, tolerance 1e-12, minimum 78/312 calendar
weeks. H63/H252 receive no inferential p-value. A zero/near-zero self-normalizer
is undefined, not infinite evidence.

The scalar SN asymptotic p-value uses the independent Brownian-bridge limit,
not a Gaussian/t p-value attached to the calibrated interval. Craig's Gaussian
tail identity and the bridge Laplace transform give
`2/pi * integral[0,pi/2] sqrt(a/sinh(a)) dtheta`, `a=sqrt(pivot)/sin(theta)`.
The deterministic integral uses fixed numerical tolerance. These are explicitly
**asymptotic development p-values**, without claimed small-tail finite-sample
calibration. The inherited interval critical 66.57 is retained: its Brownian-bridge
limit tail is about .025012, not assumed to equal .05. Nomination additionally
requires the calibrated primary interval's lower bound above zero.

BY q=.10 is applied within family to every registered H21/H126 primary spread.
Holm .05 spans all 30 add/remove cells, and separately all six interactions.
Blocked/descriptive p-values remain p=1 slots, so scope never shrinks after viewing
results. Level 2 must improve both paired demeaned-target MSE and rank-weighted
spread: its p-value is the maximum of the two. X1–X5 preserve their registered
negative controls, with fixed seed/date-hash permutations for X2/X5; X6 remains
descriptive. Residual projections are signal-date OLS on B4 percentiles/indicators,
with fixed .5 signal-only imputation. Conditional Fama–MacBeth date slopes are OLS
with the same controls, NW descriptive errors; an unidentifiable slope is withheld.

B0–B4 definitions are unchanged. Predictive models use fixed ridge penalty 1,
weighted mean squared error plus squared-coefficient penalty and an unpenalized
intercept. Each date has equal training weight and names share its weight equally.
Annual expanding fits start evaluation in 2016; training targets must exit strictly
before the cutoff 21 KR sessions before that year's first weekly signal. At least
104 past training dates and 30 names/date are required. H252/2016 has only 99 past
calendar targets and H252/2026 has no mature evaluation target, so both are
predeclared no-fit cells. The maximum planned predictive fits are **574**.

Percentiles use measured signal-time cross-sections before outcome gates. Imputation
medians and scaling are fitted only on past training rows, with one fixed missingness
indicator per column. All-missing training columns stay inactive at evaluation.
The 44.3% B4 complete-case issue is not a 55.7% forced sample deletion: any row
with at least one measured B4 column can be forecast. Forecasts also cover invalid
outcome names; the outcome mask applies only to comparisons. Paired base/augmented
metrics use identical row IDs and publish common counts/digests. Training-only
redundancy averages within-date pairwise Spearman equally across dates and connects
absolute correlations >=.8. It never selects features from evaluation outcomes.

## Economics and verdicts

Level 4 triggers automatically only after the frozen primary Level 1 BY, calibrated
interval, stability and Level 2 add-family gates. Conditional support additionally
requires its corrected interaction and negative-control comparison and an eligible
untested component; it cannot bypass earlier levels or create extra portfolios.

The diagnostic top-tercile equal-weight basket and a maximum-five-slot long-only
book are evaluated on predetermined nonoverlapping H-session calendar blocks.
Slots weigh 20%, maximum two per classified industry; empty slots earn zero-yield
cash or passive 069500.KS in separate policies. Zero stocks is valid. Slot entries
must have a positive chronological forecast exceeding frozen round-trip costs,
meet signal tradability and proxy median-traded-value capacity, and belong to the
oriented top tercile. Capital is KRW50m, each stock order <=1% of the trailing
60-session median proxy value. KRW50m is initial capital; later capacity tests use
capital compounded by prior net block returns. Unpriceable prior NAV blocks all
subsequent blocks. Exit capacity failure blocks an executable claim. Each 20% slot
is a cash budget including entry fees: asset notional is budget/(1+entry fee).
Passive fallback and the passive reference use the same funded-entry convention.
Entry selection uses signal-date fees/tax only; realized exit uses the effective
exit-date tax. No future tax change is used to qualify an entry. All policies share
predetermined anchors; an unpriceable policy blocks the joint comparison even when
isolated, explicitly bounded diagnostics remain available.
There is no optimizer, leverage or extra cost saving credited to a liquidity alpha.

Commission is 5bp/side and half spread 6bp/side. Sell tax follows the repository's
dated schedule: 30bp before 2019-06-03; 25bp then; 23bp from 2021; 20bp from 2023;
18bp from 2024; 15bp from 2025; 20bp from 2026. Benchmark cost 15bp each way is an
assumption. Every block is liquidated, so turnover and both entry/exit bills are
visible. Daily downside volatility, full path drawdown, capacity, concentration,
gross/net/excess returns and costs are reported. Paired circular 126-session,
499-draw bootstraps are descriptive only, never new nomination tests.

A/B/C/E/F/G arithmetic contributions reconcile exactly. D is a **nested** descriptive
risk-factor explanation (B4 columns plus the registered F01), not another additive
credit for the same C return. Market invested weight and descriptive beta are
reported. The existing `selection_value.decompose_edge` separately reports arithmetic
edge, geometric edge and compounding; its arithmetic book/benchmark edge is not
called individual stock selection. Only C (stock minus leave-one-out industry)
can support that claim, and regression intercepts cannot establish alpha.

Benchmark remains 069500.KS. No definition-compatible authoritative reconciliation
input is pinned, so absolute economic/investability claims stay
`BLOCKED_UNVERIFIED` in this registration. This does not erase within-date factor
information. The bounded future reconciliation rule is every same-session annual
return difference <=.50pp; no automatic retrieval or correction is performed here.
No candidate can receive `INDEPENDENT_DEVELOPMENT_SUPPORT` while this claim gate
remains unverified. Zero nominations is a valid result, not a reason to rerun.

Verdicts are exactly BLOCKED, NO_DEVELOPMENT_EVIDENCE, UNSTABLE, REDUNDANT,
NOT_ECONOMIC, INDEPENDENT_DEVELOPMENT_SUPPORT. All eventual support is development
only. Phase D closes Korean historical exploration regardless of result; independent
confirmation requires genuinely prospective preregistered signals. US work remains
outside this study.

## Execution and bounded recovery

The CLI defaults to outcome-free `validate`; `synthetic` uses invented SYN security
IDs and a distinct source identity. A real permit requires a verified permanent
lock receipt. The formal workflow has **workflow_dispatch only**, no PR/merge
trigger, and defaults to validation. This PR contains no authorization file.

Order: actual clean merged main -> explicit owner-committed authorization matching
spec/code manifest -> no prior result/artifact -> no permanent lock -> exact Phase B Git proxy
snapshot -> checksums/readiness/calendar/matrix -> pinned runtime -> synthetic checks
-> writable persistence probe -> recheck main and lock -> atomic permanent lock ->
first permitted outcome access -> integrated study -> immutable result/provenance/
checksums -> STOP. Concurrent dispatches are serialized; GitHub ref creation is
atomic. A failed network, input digest, runtime, import or synthetic preflight cannot
consume the study. Lock creation is conservatively consumed even if receipt writing
or the first outcome call fails. A partial lock claim is also consumed.

Before lock, failures may be repaired without outcomes and a new outcome-free
preflight may be attempted. After lock, no unlock/retry/model change/rethreshold is
allowed. Only recovery of **already persisted exact bytes** is permitted. A failed
attempt produces consumed-failure provenance, not invented study results. Uncertain
lock status requires reading GitHub refs before any recovery. Post-outcome diagnostic
work requires separate authorization, has an exploratory label, and cannot change
the result or become a second experiment. No unbounded retry or automatic scope cut.

Python 3.11.16; pinned runtime packages plus threadpoolctl3.7.0/joblib1.6.0; one worker
and BLAS/OpenMP thread. The full version set, platform and processor are recorded.
Maximum 574 predictive/transform fits, 52,122 outcome-free projections and 52,122
descriptive date slopes; at most 492 economic policies and 245,508 bootstrap draws
(499 per triggered policy). No bootstrap runs for untriggered policies. Memory limit
8GiB, overall in-process wall budget 5,400 seconds, feature preparation estimated
30min, scientific execution 45min, persistence margin15min. Registered resource
limits fail explicitly; engineering scope is never dropped to fit a budget.

The output schema fixes locations for label validity, attrition, every Level 1
statistic/stability/missingness, matched Level 2 comparisons, residuals/slopes,

Writes are exclusive/immutable with fsync and checksum artifacts, suitable for later

not an additional uncorrected significance claim. A family increment does not
establish that every member contributes unique information against every other
member; full removal, residual and redundancy evidence remain separate readings.
