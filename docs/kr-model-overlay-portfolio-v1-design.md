# KR model / overlay / portfolio v1 scientific design

**DEVELOPMENT_ON_OUTCOME_EXPOSED_KR_HISTORY.** History through 2026-09-14 was exposed by closed v5. This study may later train/evaluate under one fixed development protocol but can never call its result independent confirmation. v5 is not reopened. It motivates only the already-documented high-level lessons; no diagnostic result sets a threshold or weight.

The primary question is whether small PIT Value, Quality and Catalyst families can predict six-to-twelve-month benchmark-relative opportunities and support a realistic concentrated book. H126 primary; H252 secondary, descriptive and unable to rescue H126. No H21 head, no Kelly, no neural models, no hyperparameter search.

## Model

Raw registry: the eleven fields in the data audit. Individual raw fields and missingness are retained. Equal-date training weights give each training signal date total weight one. Reuse `TrainTransformer`: signed-log1p accounting and downside ratios; relative126/momentum121 and already-logged ADV unlogged; training-weighted median/IQR, nonfinite→missing, missing→training median + indicator, all-training-missing constituent omitted with disclosure. Before any imputation, each PRIMARY stock observation requires valueObserved, qualityObserved and catalystObserved: at least one finite raw VALUE, QUALITY and CATALYST constituent each. Their conjunction is coreFamilyObserved. Every constituent is not required; missing indicators remain within otherwise eligible rows. Annual full-PIT-denominator coreFamilyObserved coverage must reach the frozen 20% accounting-family floor; failure is DATA_INSUFFICIENT and cannot be weakened after outcomes. Training also requires an active constituent in every registered family. No whole-history/cross-future normalization, sector ranking or winsorization.

Family score = arithmetic average of its active training-standardized constituent values. Missing row values contribute standardized zero, with their missing indicators still in the regression. Scores are reported, not additional redundant linear regressors. Each family has equal constituent orientation: higher is cheaper/better/improving/lower risk or more liquid. Risk score is only a model predictor; portfolio sizing separately uses actual downside volatility.

Four interactions: max(Value,0)×max(Quality,0); V+×C+; Q+×C+; V+×Q+×C+. This prevents two bad scores making a positive “cheap AND good” term. Interaction median/IQR is also trained only. Ridge(alpha=10, fit_intercept=True, solver='svd') on active raw z-values, their missing indicators, and four interactions. H126/H252 fit separately. Shallow HGB: max_iter100, learning_rate.05, max_depth2, max_leaf_nodes7, min_samples_leaf20, l2_regularization10, early_stopping=False, random_state42; descriptive only. No classification head.

Annual refit on the first fixed weekly KR signal of each year; expanding chronological history; minimum36months,104 matured dates,10 names/date. Exact endpoint `< refit cutoff`, never merely an earlier signal. All later folds must be READY after first READY; no endDate/data-availability-dependent anchors. Weekly signals use the existing regional `weekly_grid`.

Baselines: date-weighted unconditional training mean and Ridge10 on relative126. The same date/name endpoints compare them. Outcome-free source gates run first. Exact inherited v5 index target = stock `Close[exit]/Close[next-session-close]-1` minus 069500.KS same-endpoint ratio, for H126 or H252 further KR sessions. This is adjusted-index relative return with **partial observed distributions**, not a promise of complete shareholder total return. Existing terminal eligibility is reused post-label, never as a stock-selection screen. MODEL training and metrics use only MATURED + ELIGIBLE rows with finite returns and observed core families. Unresolved/ineligible terminal observations are excluded and diagnosed by year/status/raw missingness, never zero/imputed. Apply ten eligible names per date and the existing 104 training/evaluation-date depth; a few unresolved rows do not invalidate sufficient remaining model evidence. Ex-ante predictions do not filter on later terminal eligibility; unresolved held terminal economics withhold the portfolio path. No zero imputation or ex-post winner-only path.

## Overlay

Only benchmark observed-close state: Close < SMA200; annualized trailing std63 >25%. Adverse flag count0/1/2 → multiplier1.00/0.70/0.40. Fixed25% is an ex-ante annual risk-budget convention, not a v5 regime fit. Missing overlay history fails the gate. No macro-based reorder or stock filter, no leverage. ## Portfolio

Up to five highest primary H126 predictions strictly above fixed ex-ante round-trip cost0.006, ticker-ascending ties. This is the zero-net-outside-option sign rule after the frozen conservative fixed cost, not an optimized hurdle. Market signal quote must be traded, ADV60 ≥ KRW3bn,126-return downside-vol ≥1% annual; no modern sector filter. Training/coverage gates do not choose top-k.

Inverse-downside-vol primary sizing. Proportional water filling under single-name30% and executable per-name allocation capacity1%×ADV / fixed reference NAV KRW100m. Unallocatable budget remains cash. Overlay scales the already-selected/base-sized book, does not reorder it. No sector cap (DEFERRED_BY_PIT_SECTOR_HISTORY); sector concentration acknowledged. Zero through five holdings valid. 30% applies to target allocations; between rebalances market drift may exceed it, is reported, and is not secretly sold daily.

Weekly signals; controlled rebalance every21 KR sessions from the fixed2015-01-01 origin, next eligible session CLOSE following the preceding complete weekly signal. Existing selected names are retained between rebalances, no daily model trade. At an anchor all executable targets are retargeted; no tuned hysteresis. Actual zero-volume/missing execution quote defers that name's order. Liquidity limit permits partial fills. After executable sells, overlayTargetGross = riskMultiplier and availableNewBuyGross = max(0, overlayTargetGross - actualPostSellGross). Costs and post-cost NAV also constrain buys. If partial/deferred exits leave gross above target, buy nothing; keep the actual holdings without inventing sales. Record overlayTargetGross, realizedGross, overlayConstraintBinding and numeric overlayExcessDueToDeferredExit. Unexecuted exits do not finance new buys. Suspended existing holdings persist; carry is allowed only for an observed zero-volume quote, not a missing delisted name. Unknown corporate-action economics block path publication rather than dropping a name. This intentionally inherits the unresolved data limitation; v1 does not invent successor returns.

## Costs and cash

Primary fixed buy15bp (commission5+slippage10); sell45bp (same15+conservative levy buffer30). These are explicit assumptions for every historical date, **not statutory historical schedules**. Additional impact5bp at1% participation, scaled sqrt(trade KRW/ADV / .01). One-way turnover=(buys+sells)/2 of NAV; initial entry cost charged once. Stress×2 and×3 descriptive, cannot choose a better primary. Reference NAV100m initializes the reference account and remains the sizing capacity scenario. Actual participation and impact use executed notional: pre-trade account NAV times the actual amount change, with the 1% ADV cap enforced on that notional. Deterministic bisection first solves executable sells against post-sell NAV, then the largest common buy fraction consistent with post-cost NAV, overlay gross and cash funding. Stocks retain their actual currency amounts; cash pays all fees. Post-cost NAV equals holdings plus cash, cash stays nonnegative, and no leverage is introduced. No post-cost weight approximation remains. Cash earns zero nominal KRW (conservative assumption); Sharpe/Sortino explicitly zero-cash-rate, not measured risk-free-return claims. Net is the exact holdings-plus-cash account less funded costs. Gross holds the SAME executed stock notionals, with saved fees retained in zero-rate cash; both paths receive identical currency stock P&L. Gross is not a separate frictionless rebalanced strategy.

## Evidence, state and diagnostics

H126 primary model requires positive date-balanced MSE improvement against BOTH mean and momentum, plus positive mean rank-weighted spread. H126 net portfolio requires positive full-span net benchmark excess AND both chronological halves positive. Gates/depth and complete endpoints/path precede these. All conjuncts → DEVELOPMENT_CANDIDATE, permission only to consider prospective validation. If all model conjuncts nonpositive OR full net excess≤0 → DEVELOPMENT_REJECT; otherwise DEVELOPMENT_INCONCLUSIVE. Undefined/incomplete data → DATA_INSUFFICIENT; machinery failure → INFRASTRUCTURE_ERROR. These development screens use point-estimate signs, carry NO confidence/pass claim and no calibrated significance label. H252, HGB, equal-weight-same-names and momentum-only portfolios, cost stress, coefficients and convergence cannot rescue the state.

Minimum evaluation104 matured dates and252 continuous portfolio sessions. Primary model metrics computed only after authorization: MSE contrasts, rank-weighted spread. Primary portfolio later includes cumulative gross/net/benchmark growth, CAGR only span≥1year, annual/downside volatility, zero-cash-rate Sharpe/Sortino, max drawdown, turnover/cost drag, daily benchmark hit fraction, holdings, concentration/cash and overlay records. No historical value of any of these was computed in this PR.

Auxiliary convergence is separate: entry vs H126 exit total-book/cap proxy change, log book growth and log(cap/book) expansion with identical statement/security basis and positive values. It explicitly includes price mechanics, share issuance/class changes and NCI effects; distribution contribution is UNRESOLVED/None. An arithmetic identity for market-cap/book movement is not perfect stock-return decomposition. No sector-relative convergence exists here. Descriptive coefficient stability and family-score/concordance tables are not ablation tournaments. Family ablation refits/ALE are deferred, not silently added after outcomes.

A substantive execution closes v1. No post-outcome cap/top-k/cost/feature/horizon/overlay/model rescue or rerun under this name. Future changes require a new version and remain development on the exposed historical sample.

Deferred/partial exits continue to occupy holding slots. New entries are admitted only into remaining slots in the unchanged model rank, preserving at most five actual holdings even during suspensions. Unfilled cash remains cash; deferred sells never finance purchases.
