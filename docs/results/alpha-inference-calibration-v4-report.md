## Interpretation

At 2,000 replicates PASS needs an observed coverage of at least 0.960 and FAIL at most 0.931, so a true coverage a little above the 0.95 floor is expected to be INCONCLUSIVE a large share of the time (this operating characteristic was published with the v4 protocol before the run). This is a limit on Monte Carlo precision, not evidence that the method under-covers.
