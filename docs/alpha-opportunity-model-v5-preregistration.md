## Model, target and evaluation contract

Inherited unchanged from the sealed v4 (each value copied with a digest and verified
on load): Ridge alpha 10 (primary), Logistic C 1, the shallow HGB, no search;
target = stock minus benchmark forward total return, next-session-close entry, H = 21
and 126 sessions; expanding annual refit, weekly prediction, equal date weights,
exact maturity purge, training-only transforms, the existing PIT universe, the
20-session traded-at-all guard, the dated KR cost schedule, label eligibility policy.
Features: the six price/attention fields for both horizons; H126 adds `assetGrowthPct`
and `debtGrowthPct`. **Nothing was added.**

Baselines on identical dates: B0 training mean, B1 zero, B2 Ridge on `relative126`,
B3 non-accounting Ridge, **B4 full Ridge (sole primary model)**, B5 HGB. H21 has no
accounting field, so B3 and B4 coincide.

**Primary claim** `CREDIBLE_INCREMENTAL_EXPECTED_RETURN` (per horizon), all at 97.5%
calendar-time self-normalised intervals: `pairedMseImprovement` B4 vs B0 lower bound
above 0, B4 vs B2 lower bound above 0, and `rankWeightedSpread` lower bound above 0.
PASS = all three; FAIL = any conjunct's upper bound below 0; INCONCLUSIVE = defined,
none refuted, one contains zero; DATA_INSUFFICIENT = gates, depth (at least 78 weekly
signal dates for H21, 312 for H126), label counts or an undefined interval;
INFRASTRUCTURE_ERROR = no complete result. Overall uses the calibrated precedence
(INFRASTRUCTURE_ERROR, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE, DATA_INSUFFICIENT, PASS); PASS needs both.

A secondary, conditional `ECONOMIC_CANDIDATE_FINDING` is evaluated only after a
primary PASS (`selectedMinusUniverse` and net `selectedMean` lower bounds above 0, at
least 52 selected dates, positive in both chronological halves). It is not an
investable or "economically meaningful" claim: the edge margin, notional, cash ADV and
slippage remain blocked.

## rankIC versus rankWeightedSpread (decided before any outcome)

`rankWeightedSpread` is the **confirmatory** ordering statistic; Spearman `rankIC` is
**descriptive only** and cannot pass or fail a claim. This is an explicit pre-outcome
revision of the earlier expectation that a positive rank-IC lower bound would be
required. Reason: rank IC is not linear in returns and so cannot be attributed to
calendar weeks; no completed calibration covers it, while `rankWeightedSpread` is
linear with signal-date weights and passed the calibration. The calibration itself said
this adoption was a v5 design decision it did not make; it is made here, in the frozen
file, not after a result.

## Other pre-outcome revisions (decisions ledger D2–D8 in the spec)

- Inference: the v4 moving-block bootstrap is replaced by the calibrated interval (no
  seed, block or draw count).
- Claims: v4's omnibus A–E gates are replaced by the structure above (design review
  R02, R03, R11).
- **`ocfToNetIncomePct` is removed from KR H126; nothing is added.** The design review
  required the cash-conversion state contract to be approved or the field excluded
  before outcomes; a new contract would be new feature construction, and the sealed
  ratio flips sign on a loss and conflates loss, absence and ratio. `debtGrowthPct` is
  reported as total-liabilities growth (values unchanged).
- Start rule (inherited): H126 starts at the first year, 2016 or later, whose accounting
  coverage passes the 0.2 floor, and stays gated. This is a pre-label input fact.

## One authorized execution

`Alpha opportunity model v5 execution` (`alpha-opportunity-model-v5-execution.yml`) is
`workflow_dispatch`, refuses any ref but `main`, has `mode: verify` (outcome-free) and
`mode: execute` (needs the operator's authorization file, refuses if a result artifact
exists, verifies first). **In this change `--execute` refuses with
`NO_V5_EXECUTION_HARNESS_IN_THIS_PR`.** The reviewed harness (labels, folds, B0–B5
fits, the statistics, the calibrated interval, its own synthetic tests) is a separate
later change kept outside the sealed closure, as v4's was. A complete substantive
result closes v5: no reseeding, threshold, feature, model, horizon, benchmark or
universe change or rerun; a retry is only for a run with no complete artifact after an
outcome-independent repair.

## Limits

Historical discovery on an already-exposed sample, not an untouched holdout; a PASS
needs prospective confirmation and a negative result does not prove no signal. The
first run may stop at a pre-label gate or find H126 shallower than 312 signal weeks;
both are reported outcomes. Complete outcome blindness is **not** certified: earlier
sessions exposed embedded research narratives, and none was used to make a choice.
