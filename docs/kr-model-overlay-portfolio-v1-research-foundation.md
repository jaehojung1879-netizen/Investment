# KR model-overlay-portfolio v1 research foundation

Design reviewed on 2026-09-30. No new-study historical outcomes were opened or calculated. Prior research narratives embedded in required AGENTS/README reads are already exposed; this is DEVELOPMENT, not an untouched confirmation sample. Literature motivates hypotheses, not a promise that a five-stock Korean portfolio captures published diversified factor premia. The quantities and parameters below were chosen from economic definitions and risk conventions, never from a v1 return comparison.

| Verified primary research | Design implication and limitation |
|---|---|
| Fama & French (2015), [A five-factor asset pricing model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304405X14002323) | Value, profitability and investment are separate economics. The small accounting/trend architecture is motivated by this distinction; no claim to replicate FF factors or their sector treatment. |
| Piotroski (2000), [Value Investing](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=249455) | Cheapness alone is insufficient; cash generation and improvement contextualize distress. A full F-score is NOT manufactured from missing concepts. |
| Novy-Marx (2013; earlier working paper 2010), [The Other Side of Value](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=1598056) | Profitability should complement value. Gross profitability is deferred: the repaired snapshot does not identify gross profit. Net-income/assets and OCF/assets are explicitly different proxies. |
| Asness, Frazzini & Pedersen (2019; working paper 2018), [Quality Minus Junk](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Quality-Minus-Junk) | Quality has profitability, growth, safety and payout dimensions. This registry covers cash conversion/profitability and a limited improvement signal, not a complete QMJ replication. |
| Hou, Xue & Zhang, [Replicating Anomalies](https://www.nber.org/papers/w23394) | Replication and investability can materially change apparent anomaly evidence. Keep the registry, controls, costs and development state fixed; do not search a factor zoo. |
| McLean & Pontiff (2016), [Does Academic Research Destroy Stock Return Predictability?](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jofi.12365) | Out-of-sample/publication decay argues for honest exposed-sample status and prospective receipts. No historical “candidate” is a live validation. |
| Gu, Kelly & Xiu (2020), [Empirical Asset Pricing via Machine Learning](https://www.nber.org/papers/w25398) | Nonlinear interactions can matter, but this is not grounds for unrestricted architecture search. Fixed Ridge remains primary; fixed shallow HGB is descriptive. |
| Moreira & Muir (2017), [Volatility Managed Portfolios](https://www.nber.org/papers/w22208) | Past volatility can inform aggregate exposure. The bounded discrete risk rule is a v1 design choice, not a replication or an optimized threshold. |
| Daniel & Moskowitz (2016), [Momentum Crashes](https://www.nber.org/papers/w20439) | Trend signals can have state-dependent tail risk. Keep the stock model and risk budget separate; do not turn the v5 regime table into a ranking gate. |
| Frazzini, Israel & Moskowitz (2018), [Trading Costs](https://www.aqr.com/Insights/Research/Working-Paper/Trading-Costs) | Trade size, liquidity and execution conditions belong in net evidence. A fixed conservative levy buffer is an assumption, not an invented Korean historical tax schedule. |

Current institutional material was checked rather than assumed:

* [MSCI Quality Indexes](https://www.msci.com/indexes/group/quality-indexes), retrieved 2026-09-30, names ROE, earnings stability and leverage. These require stronger sector/concept semantics than v1 has; do not copy definitions that the sources cannot support. The [2026 methodology index](https://www.msci.com/indexes/index-resources/index-methodology) was also verified in search.
* [AQR QMJ monthly dataset description](https://www.aqr.com/insights/datasets/quality-minus-junk-factors-monthly), dated 2026-06-30: ongoing published quality framework. No factor return series was downloaded or inspected for parameter selection.
* [Robeco QI Global Value Equities](https://www.robeco.com/en-int/products/funds/isin-lu1001396420/robeco-qi-global-value-equities-i-eur), retrieved 2026-09-30: its strategy description combines value with avoidance of adverse quality/momentum exposures and unnecessary turnover. Only the qualitative implementation principle is used; no fund performance informs v1.
* [BlackRock Systematic Investing](https://www.blackrock.com/us/individual/investment-ideas/systematic-investing), institutional index/search verified, lists a 2026-07-21 factor-rotation article. Direct article content was inaccessible (403), so no detailed empirical finding is attributed to it. Its separate [factor-investing overview](https://www.blackrock.com/us/individual/investment-ideas/what-is-factor-investing) distinguishes macro and security style factors; v1 preserves that distinction.

Value × Quality × Catalyst uses positive parts of training-standardized family scores: a cheap AND profitable/improving firm gets an interaction; two poor scores do not produce an attractive interaction merely by multiplying two negatives. This is an explicit economic design choice. Price momentum is a limited convergence/catalyst proxy, not proof of a particular corporate event. Portfolio concentration sacrifices diversification, hence the 30% cap, zero-to-five admission, liquidity participation limit and cash. Published broad-factor evidence does not establish concentrated-portfolio capacity or suitability.
